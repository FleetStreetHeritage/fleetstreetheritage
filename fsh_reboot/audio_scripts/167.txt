All the original plots had very narrow frontages, typically of 15 feet, so they began to be joined together to make larger premises, and, when the newspapers arrived, a whole block could be just one building. In 1847, there were 79 separate addresses on the south side.  They contained a huge variety of small business from Carvers and Gilders to umbrella makers, and many others shown below.

By 2026, the 79 addresses had shrunk to 45, and most of them were selling fast food or drink.
