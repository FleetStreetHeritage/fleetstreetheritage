Fleet Street Heritage Walk Map The Jubilee Walkway was the first urban walkway in the world.

It is 14 miles long.

The City Loop (section 3) comes down Chancery Lane from Holborn, turns left into Fleet Street to St.

Pauls, and then goes across the Millennium Bridge, with these pavement markers at frequent intervals.  Type “Jubilee Walkway section 3 directions”

into yur search engine to get detailed instructions.
