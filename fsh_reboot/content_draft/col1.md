## Planning Permission

Planning permission was given on 29 April 2025 for the Fleet Street Heritage Wall under the sundial in Bouverie Street. The next step will be raising the funds. We hope to build the Heritage Wall this summer.

This innovative project will help make Fleet Street a really special place again — please see our planning application [leaflet](https://www.fleetstreetheritage.co.uk/Leaflet2.html) for full details.

A temporary Heritage Wall Exhibition is displayed under the sundial on Bouverie Street (EC4Y 1JU).
