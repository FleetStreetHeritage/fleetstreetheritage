Sir Edwin Lutyens (1868–1947) designed only a few famous buildings, including the Reuters building at 85 Fleet Street (1935). Reuters, a major news agency, reported historic events like Lincoln’s assassination and the Berlin Wall.
The Grade 2 Listed building has a stone façade with a bold entrance, upper floors with plain stone, and a curved top. Lutyens used perspective tricks and fine details to make it stand out.
When Reuters left in 2005, it marked the end of major news outlets on Fleet Street. Some original features, like bronze name plaques, have been removed but the building remains architecturally important.
