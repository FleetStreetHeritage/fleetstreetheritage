Charles Dickens started the Daily News in 1846 after problems with another newspaper. He wanted it to support progress, education, and fairness, but he left soon after because the work was too tiring.
The paper survived by changing its price and format and later grew quickly by using the telegraph, especially during the Franco-Prussian War.
In 1901, George Cadbury bought the paper and made it strongly Liberal and anti-war. It became influential in politics.
Over time, the Daily News merged with other papers and in 1930 became part of the News Chronicle, reaching a large national audience.
