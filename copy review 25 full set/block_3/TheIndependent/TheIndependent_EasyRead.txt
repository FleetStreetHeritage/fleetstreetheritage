The Independent was launched in 1986 as the first new quality national newspaper in over a century. Its founders wanted a fresh, independent voice, free from political bias, with the informal motto: “classic with a twist.” It quickly stood out for social and economic liberalism, bold reporting, and innovative style.
The paper has taken brave stances on major issues, including opposing the Iraq War, highlighting the refugee crisis, advocating for climate action, and campaigning for a public “Final Say” on Brexit. It has avoided celebrity-driven coverage of the monarchy and has always challenged convention.
The Independent has been a pioneer in journalism: introducing a Saturday magazine, moving from broadsheet to tabloid, prioritizing photography, launching the compact paper i, a children’s edition, and going fully digital in 2016. It has also given a platform to influential writers, from Robert Fisk and Patrick Cockburn to Helen Fielding.
Today, The Independent is a global digital brand, still fiercely independent, innovative, and closely connected to its readers.
