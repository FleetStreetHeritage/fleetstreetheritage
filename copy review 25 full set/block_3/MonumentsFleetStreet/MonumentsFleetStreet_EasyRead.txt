The church of St. Dunstan-in-the-West on Fleet Street has three notable monuments. The statue of Queen Elizabeth I, from 1586, is London’s oldest public statue and was moved here after Ludgate was demolished in 1760. Above the statue are Gog and Magog, legendary guardians who strike the bells hourly. At street level is a sculpted head of Lord Northcliffe, a press baron who supported the church.
