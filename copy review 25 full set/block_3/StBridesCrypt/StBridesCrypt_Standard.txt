St Bride’s extensive crypt is well worth a visit. It contains fragments of many of the previous buildings on the site, a collection of the finds made in the extensive post-war excavations and a large exhibition space. These pictures are examples. The first building on this site was in Roman times, and since then, there have been eight churches built here. 
The seventh was destroyed in the Great Fire of London (1666) as were 86 other churches in the City. The current church, designed by Sir Christopher Wren, was one of the 51 churches rebuilt in the City after the fire. St Bride’s was extensively damaged by bombs in 1940  and restored. The present church is fully described on another  Heritage page.
