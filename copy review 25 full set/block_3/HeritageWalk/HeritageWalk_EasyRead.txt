The walk begins at the Dragon statue on Fleet Street, marking the City of London boundary and the former site of Temple Bar. You walk along Fleet Street, passing historic buildings such as the Old Bank of England pub and Prince Henry’s Room, one of the few pre–Great Fire survivors.
The route goes through Chancery Lane and quiet courtyards like Clifford’s Inn, then past St Dunstan-in-the-West church with its famous clock figures and statues. You continue by Hoare’s Bank, one of the oldest family-owned banks in Britain, and explore narrow lanes that survived the Great Fire.
The walk passes sites linked to science, literature, and journalism, including the former home of the Royal Society, statues of John Wilkes and Dr Johnson’s cat Hodge, and historic newspaper buildings. Highlights include the Fleet Street Heritage Sundial and Magpie Alley, celebrating the printing industry.
The walk ends at Ludgate Circus, near a plaque to writer and journalist Edgar Wallace, above the buried River Fleet.
