Gresham College is a historic educational institution, founded in 1579 by Sir Thomas Gresham, a successful merchant from Norfolk. He had helped the government recover from a financial crisis and built the Royal Exchange in London. Gresham left his fortune to create a college in Bishopsgate, with seven Professors teaching Divinity, Geometry, Astronomy, Music, Law, Physic, and Rhetoric. Professors were unmarried, given a home, and paid to give free public lectures once a week. The first lecture was in 1597. Famous academics like Christopher Wren and Robert Hooke benefited from Gresham College, which contributed to important discoveries like logarithms. Despite setbacks, including the Great Fire and disputes over Latin lectures, the College survived. Since 1989, it has flourished, offering lectures from distinguished Professors, live-streamed worldwide, continuing Gresham’s vision of accessible education for all.
