Fleet Street has a number of very ancient narrow courts, mainly on the north side, and most leading through to streets beyond. Many of them have pavement plaques relating to their history.The schematic below shows the order in which these courts, and the side-streets in CAPITALS, would appear if you were walking up the hill from Ludgate Circus to the City boundary at Temple Bar. The widths of the courts are given in metres to show you quite how narrow they are.
