

Fleet Street is well known for its rich mix of architectural styles and historical buildings. Its importance comes from its location between London and Westminster, two centres of power for many centuries, and from its close links to journalism, law, banking, shops, and pubs.
This guide helps readers understand the architecture of Fleet Street and how its buildings are organised. Unlike most streets, the building numbers run in one direction on one side and back on the other. With short descriptions, photos, and useful links, the guide makes it easy to explore the architectural history of this famous street.
