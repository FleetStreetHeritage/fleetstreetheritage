This striking statue of St George stands in the courtyard of the Premier Inn on Dorset Rise. He leans forward on his horse, driving his lance into the dragon below.
Created by sculptor Michael Sandle and cast by the Morris Singer Foundry, it was the winning entry in a 1986 Unilever competition during the building’s renovation.
Unlike traditional depictions, St George is a tough working man, and the dragon is a large, serpent-like creature rather than a fire-breathing beast.
Though lesser-known due to its location, the statue is a powerful and unique part of Fleet Street’s heritage.
