The Jubilee Walkway was created in 1977 to mark Queen Elizabeth II’s Silver Jubilee. Designed by Max Nicholson, it was London’s first urban walking trail. The route started in Leicester Square, crossed Lambeth Bridge, ran along the south bank of the Thames to Tower Bridge, and returned through the City of London.
The Queen opened it on 9 June 1977. By 1994, the south bank route was fully developed as The Queen’s Walk. Later extensions marked her Golden Jubilee in 2003 and Diamond Jubilee in 2012, linking the main Olympic sites.
