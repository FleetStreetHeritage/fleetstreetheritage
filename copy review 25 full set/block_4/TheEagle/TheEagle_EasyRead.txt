Eagle was a popular boys’ magazine in the 1950s. It was created by Marcus Morris, a vicar, and Frank Hampson, an artist. They wanted to give boys exciting stories that were fun but good for them, instead of the violent American comics. Together they made Dan Dare, Pilot of the Future.
Marcus had a hard time finding a publisher and nearly went bankrupt. Finally, Hulton Press agreed to print Eagle in 1950. The first issue sold almost a million copies. The magazine was full of colorful illustrations, exciting adventures, and stories that taught good values. Boys joined the Eagle Club and read about explorers, missionaries, and heroes.
Eagle stayed very popular for many years. Other magazines followed, like Girl, Swift, and Robin. But in 1959, a new company took over, and Marcus left. Frank stayed for a while, but the magazine slowly became just a regular comic. By the 1960s, the other magazines had disappeared.
