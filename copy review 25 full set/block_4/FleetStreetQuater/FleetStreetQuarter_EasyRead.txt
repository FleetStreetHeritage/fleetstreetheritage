In February 2022, local businesses voted to create the Fleet Street Quarter Business Improvement District (BID), showing strong support for working together to make the area a thriving destination. The BID covers Fleet Street at its heart, and nearby areas such as Chancery Lane, Holborn, Ludgate Hill, and New Street Square.
The BID’s work focuses on making the area vibrant, safe, inclusive, and sustainable. Through partnerships, cultural events, marketing, and initiatives like the Green Skills Construction Hub and Urban Farmer Project, it promotes innovation, green infrastructure, and local business growth.
The BID also improves streets and public spaces, tackles crime and anti-social behaviour, and supports SMEs, creating a safe, welcoming environment for workers, visitors, and residents. The Fleet Street Quarter Ambassador team helps maintain this presence, ensuring the area is clean, secure, and inviting.
More information can be found at fleetstreetquarter.co.uk.
